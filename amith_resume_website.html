Real-time Fraud Detection System

Problem Statement: Financial institutions face significant challenges with real-time fraud, leading to substantial monetary losses and eroded customer trust. The goal was to develop a system capable of identifying and flagging suspicious transactions as they occur.

Methodology: I implemented a streaming data pipeline using Apache Kafka to ingest transaction data. A Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM) model, trained on historical fraudulent and legitimate transactions, was deployed for real-time inference. Feature engineering involved creating temporal and behavioral features.

Results & Impact: The system achieved an accuracy of 98.5% and a recall of 92% for fraudulent transactions, reducing false positives by 30%. This led to an estimated saving of $5M annually in fraud-related losses.

Learnings & Challenges: Handling imbalanced datasets was a key challenge, addressed by using techniques like SMOTE and custom loss functions. Ensuring low latency for real-time predictions required careful optimization of the model and infrastructure.

Key Technologies:

Python Scikit-learn Apache Kafka AWS EC2 Pandas

Customer Churn Prediction & Retention

Problem Statement: A telecommunications company was experiencing high customer churn, impacting revenue and market share. The objective was to predict which customers were likely to churn and to devise effective retention strategies.

Methodology: I utilized historical customer data, including usage patterns, billing information, and customer service interactions. An XGBoost classifier was trained and optimized to predict churn probability. Feature importance analysis was used to identify key drivers of churn.

Results & Impact: The model achieved an AUC score of 0.88. By identifying high-risk customers, the marketing team could implement targeted retention campaigns, leading to a 15% reduction in churn rate over six months.

Learnings & Challenges: Data quality and feature engineering from disparate data sources were crucial. Collaboration with marketing and sales teams was essential to ensure the model's output was actionable and integrated into business processes.

Key Technologies:

Python XGBoost SQL Tableau Scikit-learn

NLP-driven Sentiment Analysis for Product Reviews

Problem Statement: A large e-commerce platform needed to understand customer sentiment from thousands of product reviews to prioritize product improvements and marketing strategies. Manual analysis was infeasible.

Methodology: I designed and implemented an NLP pipeline using pre-trained Transformer models (e.g., BERT) for sentiment classification. The pipeline included data cleaning, tokenization, embedding, and fine-tuning the model on a labeled dataset of reviews.

Results & Impact: The sentiment analysis model achieved an F1-score of 0.90, accurately categorizing reviews as positive, negative, or neutral. This enabled the product team to quickly identify pain points and positive feedback, leading to a 10% increase in product ratings for improved items.

Learnings & Challenges: Handling noisy, informal text data from reviews was challenging. Fine-tuning large language models required significant computational resources, which was managed by leveraging GCP's AI Platform.

Key Technologies:

Python PyTorch Hugging Face Transformers Google Cloud Platform (GCP) NLTK
